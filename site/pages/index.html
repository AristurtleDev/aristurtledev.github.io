About

Hi, I'm Christopher Whitley, though you'll probably know me online as AristurtleDev.

I'm a software developer who enjoys building the tools, libraries, and educational resources that make game development a little easier for everyone else. While I do build games, I've discovered that I get the most satisfaction from creating the infrastructure that helps other developers build theirs.

Most of my work revolves around the MonoGame ecosystem, where I maintain open-source projects, contribute to community libraries, write tutorials, and help improve documentation. I enjoy taking complex topics and breaking them down into explanations that I wish had existed when I was learning.

This website is where I collect everything I create: open-source projects, technical articles, tutorials, experiments, and the occasional deep dive into whatever has captured my attention that week. If something I've learned can save someone else a few hours of frustration, it's probably going to end up here.
